VIRTUAL CONSULTATION

See your dentist
from home.

A virtual consultation is a video call with your dentist, instead of coming into the practice.

WHAT IT IS

A video call with your dentist, instead of coming into the practice.

Phone, tablet or computer

Live, real-time call

No travel needed

Medical aid is not accepted

Virtual consultations are self-pay only.

Consent form required

You must complete the logbox consent form with your details before the call.

HOW IT WORKS

Booking steps

  1. 1
    Open the RecoMed booking link.
  2. 2
    Select “Virtual consult” as the reason for the visit.
  3. 3
    Choose a time slot allocated to virtual consultations and register with a phone number that has WhatsApp enabled.

    This is where the payment link and follow-up links will be sent.

  4. 4
    Receive a payment link via WhatsApp.
  5. 5
    Complete payment.
  6. 6
    On successful payment, receive three things via WhatsApp:
    • Proof of payment
    • Logbox link, to give consent and enter your details. Required before the call.
    • Zoom link for the consultation
