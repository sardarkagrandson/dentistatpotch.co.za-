VIRTUAL CONSULTATION

See your dentist
from home.

A virtual consultation is a video call with your dentist, instead of coming into the practice.

WHAT IT IS

A video call with your dentist, instead of coming into the practice.

Phone, tablet or computer

Live, real-time call

No travel needed

Medical aid is not accepted

Virtual consultations are self-pay only.

Consent form required

You must complete the logbook consent form with your details before the call.

HOW IT WORKS

Times, cost and how to book.

Booking steps

  1. Open the RecoMed booking link.
  2. Select “Virtual consult” as the reason for the visit.
  3. Choose a time slot allocated to virtual consultations.
  4. Enter a phone number that has WhatsApp attached. This is how the payment link and follow-up links are sent.
  5. Receive a payment link via WhatsApp.
  6. Complete payment.
  7. On successful payment, receive three things via WhatsApp:
    • Proof of payment
    • Logbook link, to give consent and enter your details. Required before the call.
    • Zoom link for the consultation
